# Getting-started screenshot checklist

Already covered in the README: choosing an extracted library, the library
and empty-bank overview, the collection menu, and combined sound-type filtering.

Capture the following next, in roughly this order. Use a named demonstration kit
and readable sample names throughout so readers can follow one complete workflow.

| Priority | Feature | What to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Preview and sample details | Select a sound with duration, bit rate, bit depth, sample rate and channels visible, plus Show in Finder. |
| 2 | Manual pad assignment | A selected library sound assigned to A01 with its name visible. A short recording can show drag-and-drop more clearly than a still. |
| 3 | Auto-fill | A populated bank after Auto-fill Empty Pads, with the active filters and occupied-pad count visible. |
| 4 | Multiple banks | The same demonstration kit in banks A and B (two shots), showing different assignments and the selected bank. |
| 5 | Clear Bank | Before/after shots of clearing one bank while another remains populated. Use a disposable demonstration kit. |
| 6 | Save and reopen | Save Kit dialog with a friendly kit name and .marspad extension; then the reopened kit with assignments restored. |
| 7 | Native export template | Export MPC's first-use template chooser with a device-saved .xpj or project ZIP selected. This is a template, not the kit plan. |
| 8 | Exported project files | Finder showing the kit-named .xpj beside its matching _[ProjectData] folder. Expand the audio folder to show preserved sample names in pad-prefixed WAV filenames. |
| 9 | SD-card placement | Finder showing the .xpj and matching audio folder directly inside MPC-Sample/Projects. Keep this distinct from the outer local export folder. |
| 10 | MPC Sample result | A photo of the exported kit loaded on the hardware, matching the pad names/positions shown in MarsPad. |

Optional follow-ups: a filename search, Show in Finder revealing a problem sample,
the audio-only export and CSV pad map, and the version/commit footer for reporting
issues.

## Capture tips

- Keep a consistent window size and demonstration kit across the sequence.
- Move focus out of the kit-name field so its text is not highlighted unless the
  screenshot is specifically demonstrating renaming.
- Keep the relevant controls and bank letters legible; crop only if it improves
  clarity and retains context.
- Avoid showing unrelated personal folders, account details, or full private paths.
- Screenshots should show the app and filenames, not distribute commercial audio
  or personal project files.
- Note which app version the shots represent. The existing shots show v0.2.4;
  their conservative hardware-validation notice predates the later recorded tests.
